01 / THE PRINCIPLE

Good systems are defined by what they refuse to do.

An agent that moves money should never hold the key. A score should never invent the evidence it lacks. A record should prove the work without exposing the person who did it.

I design those limits first, then build the product around them — so people can trust it on the day it matters.

03 / HOW I BUILD

Say what it does. Then prove it.

I write down what a product does not do yet, right next to what it does. A claim points to evidence — a transaction, a test, a recording — or it stays off the page.

  • Money waits for a signature.The agent prepares; the person decides.
  • Evidence over inference.If a source is missing, the system says so.
  • Privacy by subtraction.Publish the outcome; keep the person off the record.
