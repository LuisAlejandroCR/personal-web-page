The site, in black and butter.

The OPAC boards, taken apart into what is actually reusable and what is not, then applied to the page you already have. The refusal slip below is the real Cerrojo exhibit from your hero — same copy, same structure, wearing the new palette.

Palette#000 · #1b1b1b · #fff · #fff9aa

Colour values carry no rights and this pairing is common. The one hard constraint: butter on white is 1.08:1 — it can never be text on a light ground, only a fill behind black type.

What this costs you

The switch is four CSS custom properties and two font-family stacks — the structure, the scroll timelines and the language toggle are untouched. What it actually costs is the warm, literary register the page has now: this direction reads engineered rather than written. That is the real decision, not the hex values.
